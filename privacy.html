Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

RAGEview is a tool that shows organizations how their posts and videos perform on social media, in one place. This page explains what information we handle and why.

What we collect

  • Your account: your email address and a scrambled (hashed) version of your password. We cannot read your password.
  • Your brands: the brand names you create in RAGEview.
  • Linked social accounts: when you press Connect and approve access, the platform gives us a secure permission token plus the account's name and ID.
  • Performance data: for linked accounts only, the titles, dates and numbers (such as views, impressions, likes, comments and shares) of recent posts and videos.

How we use it

We use this information only to show you your own reports inside RAGEview. We do not sell it, share it for advertising, or use it to build profiles. We do not read private messages, and we do not post, edit or delete anything on your accounts. We only read.

Google and YouTube data

RAGEview's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. When you link YouTube, you are also agreeing to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Who can see your data

Only you, and the people who sign in with your account. Other organizations that use RAGEview cannot see your brands or numbers. The administrator of RAGEview can view brands for support purposes. Our hosting and database providers (Vercel and Neon) store the data on our behalf.

Your choices

You can unlink any social account, or delete any brand, inside RAGEview at any time, and its stored numbers and tokens are removed. You can also revoke access from each platform's own settings. To delete your whole account and data, email us at the address below.
